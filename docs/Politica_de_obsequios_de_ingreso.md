# Política de obsequios de ingreso

Operational reference for festival entrance gifts (obsequios de ingreso). This document defines visitor-facing wording and staff procedure. It is the committed source linked from the Fast Pass PRD.

## Eligibility

- Gift eligibility is determined only by physical arrival order at a common control point.
- Entrance type (standard line or Pase Rápido) does not grant gift priority.
- Pase Rápido never jumps the gift queue or reserves promotional gifts.

## Visitor wording

Use language that separates entrance priority from gift eligibility, for example:

- Pase Rápido improves access to the venue, not access to first-arrival gifts.
- Obsequios de ingreso se entregan por orden de llegada física al punto de control, independientemente del tipo de ingreso.
- Tener Pase Rápido no garantiza ni adelanta un obsequio promocional.

Avoid implying that Fast Pass includes merchandise, gift tokens, or first-arrival priority.

## Staff procedure

1. Direct all visitors who want a gift to the shared gift control point, regardless of entrance lane.
2. Issue gifts strictly in physical arrival order at that point.
3. If gift stock runs out, communicate clearly that eligibility ended with stock, not with entrance type.
4. Do not divert Fast Pass holders ahead of the gift line as a courtesy of the product.

## Relationship to Pase Rápido

- Fast Pass may shorten or prioritize venue entry when capacity and safety allow.
- Fast Pass does not change gift policy, gift inventory rules, or gift messaging.
